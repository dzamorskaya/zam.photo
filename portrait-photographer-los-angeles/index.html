A few answers.

What should I wear?+

Bring up to two looks that feel like you. We can discuss colours, textures and visual references while planning.

Can you help choose a location?+

Yes. We discuss location ideas and choose one setting for the session. Studio rental, if needed, is paid separately.

Do I need modelling experience?+

No. You’ll receive direction and posing guidance throughout the session.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. This includes colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final image preparation. Complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.
