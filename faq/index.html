Before we begin

Good questions.

Where are you based?+

I’m based in Los Angeles, California, and available for selected travel projects. Include your location when you inquire.

Do I need modelling experience?+

No. I help with direction, posing and visual planning throughout the shoot.

How far ahead should I book?+

Send your preferred date as early as you can. Availability is confirmed personally before booking.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. Colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final preparation are included; complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.

How are final images delivered?+

Final photographs are delivered digitally. Portrait delivery is 5–7 days, headshots 3–5 days, and branding and creative editorial sessions up to 7 days.

Is a studio included?+

Studio rental, if needed, is paid separately.

How do I book?+

Send an inquiry with the type of shoot, your preferred timing and your ideas. I’ll reply with availability, pricing and the next steps. Your booking and final scope are confirmed personally.
