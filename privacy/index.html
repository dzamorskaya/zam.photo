Privacy.

Contacting Daria

The inquiry form sends your name, email, shoot preferences, message and any optional project details to Daria through FormSubmit. These details are used to respond and plan your project. FormSubmit processes submissions and sends email notifications; its privacy terms apply. Spam protection may involve Google reCAPTCHA. The form keeps a temporary draft in this browser tab so you can recover your details if submission is interrupted. The draft expires after two hours and is removed after confirmation or when the tab is closed. Email and Instagram remain available for direct contact. Their own privacy terms also apply.

Hosting and technical data

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate its service.

Optional analytics

No analytics service is currently configured on this website. If analytics is enabled in the future, it will require your consent before loading. Your choice is stored in this browser.
