A few answers.

How is commercial usage priced?+

Usage and licensing are scoped for each project. Include the intended channels, usage period and territory in your brief.

Can you work with creative teams and agencies?+

Yes. Share your team, creative direction and production requirements so we can define responsibilities and scope.

Can you quote custom production?+

Yes. Quotes depend on production, deliverables and usage. Include your timeline, location, campaign type and required final assets.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. This includes colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final image preparation. Complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.
