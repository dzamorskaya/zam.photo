Daria Zamorskaia

Image-maker.

Daria Zamorskaia is a Los Angeles-based photographer specializing in portrait, fashion, editorial, headshot and personal branding photography. Her work combines cinematic storytelling, strong composition and a contemporary editorial aesthetic. She works with individuals, models, artists, creatives and brands throughout Los Angeles.

My approach brings an editorial sensibility to real people. From the first conversation to the final retouch, the aim is a photograph with presence — and a point of view.
