A few answers.

Do you photograph actors?+

Yes. Headshots are available for actors, models, entrepreneurs, creatives and professionals. Tell me how the images will be used so we can plan the right direction.

How many looks can I bring?+

The starting package includes up to two looks in a session of up to 45 minutes.

When will I receive my headshots?+

The package includes three professionally retouched final images, delivered digitally in 3–5 days. Additional retouched images are $25 each.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. This includes colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final image preparation. Complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.
