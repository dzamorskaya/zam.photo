A few answers.

Can we plan content for my website and social media?+

Yes. Planning includes a shot list and horizontal and vertical formats, from portraits and website banners to working moments and lifestyle imagery.

Can I include different outfits and locations?+

The starting session includes 2–3 looks. We plan locations around your shot list and the session length; additional production is discussed before booking.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. This includes colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final image preparation. Complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.
