A few answers.

Who is the starting package for?+

The $600 starting package is for models and creative editorials. It is not a commercial campaign rate or a commercial image licence.

Can a brand commission a lookbook or campaign?+

Yes. Send a commercial brief with your deliverables, production needs and intended usage for a separate quote.

Is retouching included?+

Professional retouching is included in all final selected images. This includes colour correction, skin-tone adjustments, temporary imperfections and final image preparation. Complex beauty retouching is scoped separately.

For brands & designers.

Lookbooks, campaigns and commercial imagery are scoped around production, deliverables and usage. The model and creative editorial package does not include commercial licensing.
