A few answers.

Where do you photograph?+

I am based in Los Angeles. Sessions take place in the city and surrounding areas; travel can be discussed for a specific project.

I have never been photographed professionally. Is that okay?+

Yes. Portrait sessions include direction and posing guidance. We can discuss the mood and purpose of the photographs before the shoot.

Is retouching included?+

Photography packages include the retouched image selection specified in the session details. Standalone professional retouching is also available; contact me with the images and scope.

How do I book?+

Email dzamorskaya@icloud.com or send a message to @zam.photo on Instagram. Tell me the type of shoot, your preferred timing and any ideas or references.

What is the difference between headshots and personal branding?+

Headshots focus on a clear portrait for professional or casting use. Personal branding develops a wider visual library: portraits, working moments, lifestyle images and different formats for your business.

Can brands request a custom quote?+

Yes. Send your brief, deliverables, timeline and intended image usage. Production, licensing and post-production can then be scoped for the project.
